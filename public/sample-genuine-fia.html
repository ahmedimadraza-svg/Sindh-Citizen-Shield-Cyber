NATIONAL CYBER CRIME INVESTIGATION AGENCY (NCCIA)

PUBLIC CYBER SECURITY ADVISORY - REF: CCW/2026/ADV-09

SUBJECT: CITIZEN WARNING REGARDING OTP FRAUD & BENEFICIARY IMPERSONATION

Citizens of Pakistan are hereby cautioned that government departments, state banks, and welfare programmes (BISP 8171) NEVER contact citizens from private 11-digit mobile numbers or request One-Time Passwords (OTPs) or banking PINs.

  • Do NOT share 4-digit or 6-digit OTP codes received via SMS with any caller.
  • Official BISP communications are transmitted strictly via Shortcode 8171.
  • Report cyber fraud immediately to FIA Cyber Crime Helpline 1991 or via web portal at complaint.fia.gov.pk.
ISSUED IN PUBLIC INTEREST BY CYBER CRIME WING, FIA HEADQUARTERS, ISLAMABAD. HELPLINE: 1991 | WEB: FIA.GOV.PK
